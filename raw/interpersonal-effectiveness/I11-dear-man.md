# DEAR MAN

Asserting yourself can feel uncomfortable, whether you're asking for what you want, declining a request, or standing up for yourself without becoming aggressive or withdrawn. DEAR MAN is a fundamental tool within DBT's interpersonal effectiveness framework that addresses these challenges.

This skill provides structure for communicating your needs clearly, respectfully, and with confidence. It applies across numerous contexts from requesting a salary increase to establishing boundaries with loved ones to asking for support from a partner. The technique emphasizes effectiveness rather than manipulation, ensuring your message gets heard and understood.

## DEAR MAN Meaning

The acronym divides into two parts:

**DEAR** represents what you communicate:

- D: Describe
- E: Express
- A: Assert
- R: Reinforce

**MAN** represents how you communicate it:

- M: Mindful
- A: Appear confident
- N: Negotiate

## DEAR: What You Say

### D - Describe the situation

Present only objective facts without judgment. What occurred? What is the specific problem?

Example: "We planned to split household responsibilities, but I've handled laundry for three consecutive weeks."

### E - Express your feelings and opinions

Share your emotional experience using "I" language, avoiding blame or criticism.

Example: "I feel overwhelmed and undervalued."

### A - Assert your needs or ask clearly

State your request directly and specifically.

Example: "I'd like us to return to alternating laundry weeks."

### R - Reinforce why it matters

Describe the positive outcomes or express gratitude.

Example: "This would help me feel supported, and our household would run more smoothly."

## MAN: How You Say It

### M - Mindful

Maintain concentration on your objective. Resist tangential discussions or deflections. If defensiveness emerges, remain grounded and on point.

Use the "broken record" technique: calmly repeat your request if necessary.

Example: "I understand, but I'd like to continue discussing how we split chores."

### A - Appear confident

Confidence can be performed even when not felt internally. This includes:

- Consistent eye contact
- Measured tone
- Upright posture
- Avoiding apologies for reasonable needs

### N - Negotiate

Demonstrate flexibility and willingness to collaborate. Propose alternatives or explore solutions together.

Example: "If laundry isn't your preference, would you handle dishes while I manage laundry instead?"

## Applying it in Real Life

**Scenario:** Your roommate's television volume interferes with your work calls.

**DEAR MAN execution:**

- Describe: "When the television plays loudly during my work meetings..."
- Express: "I become anxious and lose concentration."
- Assert: "I need the volume reduced during my work hours."
- Reinforce: "This helps my focus and reduces friction between us."
- Mindful: Remain composed regardless of their reaction.
- Appear confident: Keep your voice steady and measured.
- Negotiate: "Could we establish quiet hours or use headphones?"

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this technique too pushy?**

No. DEAR MAN emphasizes respectfulness and clarity. Your needs matter; everyone deserves to advocate for themselves.

**What if someone reacts defensively?**

The "Mindful" and "Negotiate" components address this. While you cannot control their response, maintaining composure keeps discussions productive.

**Must I use all seven steps?**

Not necessarily. Informal situations may require streamlining. The goal is effective communication, not rigid adherence to structure.

**Can this work in digital communication?**

Absolutely. This framework translates well to written formats, provided your tone remains respectful and direct without sarcasm.

**What if I don't achieve my desired outcome?**

You won't always succeed. However, the technique builds clarity and personal integrity, strengthening assertiveness gradually through practice.

**What if I forget what to say?**

Consider using [GIVE](https://dialecticalbehaviortherapy.com/interpersonal-effectiveness/give-skill/) or [FAST](https://dialecticalbehaviortherapy.com/interpersonal-effectiveness/fast-skill/) beforehand to stabilize yourself emotionally and communicate with composure despite stammering.

**What if emotional overwhelm prevents me from speaking?**

Begin with emotion regulation. [ABC PLEASE](https://dialecticalbehaviortherapy.com/emotion-regulation/abc-please/) can reduce emotional intensity, preparing you to communicate effectively.

**What if conflict scares me?**

Conflict anxiety is legitimate. Pair DEAR MAN with [Middle Path](https://dialecticalbehaviortherapy.com/supplementary/middle-path/) to honor both your needs and theirs simultaneously.

# ABC PLEASE: DBT Skills for Emotional Regulation

## What ABC PLEASE Stands For

### ABC: Building a Solid Emotional Foundation

- A: Accumulate positive experiences
- B: Build mastery
- C: Cope ahead

### PLEASE: Take Care of Your Body So It Supports Your Mind

- PL: Treat Physical illness
- E: Eat balanced meals
- A: Avoid mood-altering substances
- S: Sleep enough
- E: Exercise regularly

## Core Concept

According to the resource, when you're "constantly tired, sick, disconnected, or physically run down," your capacity to manage emotions diminishes significantly. ABC PLEASE functions as a foundational DBT skill designed to prevent emotional instability by strengthening your baseline resilience.

## ABC Section Details

### Accumulate Positive Experiences

Engage in activities creating joy and connection—start with small actions like watching videos or listening to music, while also pursuing meaningful long-term goals.

### Build Mastery

Challenge yourself appropriately through tasks like learning recipes or completing avoided projects to generate feelings of accomplishment and control.

### Cope Ahead

Mentally rehearse handling future stressful situations by visualizing successful outcomes and imagining yourself using DBT techniques.

## PLEASE Section Details

### Treat Physical Illness

Address health issues since your body and mind function interdependently.

### Eat Balanced Meals

Maintain regular eating patterns with nutritious foods to avoid blood sugar fluctuations mimicking emotional crashes.

### Avoid Mood-Altering Substances

Eliminate alcohol, drugs, and excessive caffeine or sugar that destabilize emotional balance.

### Sleep Enough

Prioritize consistent sleep schedules, as inadequate rest impairs focus, emotion regulation, and stress responses.

### Exercise Regularly

Engage in any movement—walking, stretching, or dancing—to reduce stress hormones and boost mood-regulating chemicals.

## Application Guidance

The resource suggests asking yourself daily: Did you do something joyful? Did you challenge yourself? Are you avoiding obvious stressors like sleep deprivation or skipping meals?

When these basics are maintained, other DBT skills become more accessible and effective.

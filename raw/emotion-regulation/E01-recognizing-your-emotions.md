# Recognizing Your Emotions: DBT Emotion Regulation Exercise

## Overview

This exercise from the Emotion Regulation module teaches a 6-step technique developed by Marsha Linehan to help individuals understand how emotions escalate and spiral. The technique distinguishes between primary emotions (immediate reactions) and secondary emotions (reactions to primary emotions).

## The Six-Step Process

**Step 1: Describe the Situation**
Document what happened, where it occurred, and who was involved in an emotionally overwhelming experience.

**Step 2: What Caused the Situation**
Examine your thoughts about what caused the situation, recognizing that "the way you explain the situation to yourself will shape your emotions about the situation."

**Step 3: Primary and Secondary Emotions**
Identify your initial emotion and subsequent emotional responses, plus any associated physical sensations (tension, rapid heartbeat, trembling).

**Step 4: Identify Your Urges**
Recognize what actions you wanted to take as a result of your emotions, distinguishing between urges and actual behaviors.

**Step 5: What Did You Do**
Describe the actual actions you took in response to your emotional state.

**Step 6: Outcomes**
Evaluate the longer-term consequences of your emotionally-driven actions and whether the outcome was beneficial.

## Key FAQs

The exercise differs from the mindfulness "describing emotions" module by examining the broader context including environmental factors, interpretations, urges, and outcomes. It's designed to help individuals slow emotional escalation and make healthier coping choices in future situations.

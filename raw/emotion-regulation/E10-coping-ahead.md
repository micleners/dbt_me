# DBT Coping Ahead Exercise (E10)

## Overview

The Coping Ahead exercise teaches individuals to prepare for stressful situations by planning ahead and mentally rehearsing appropriate DBT skills before encountering challenging circumstances.

## Three-Step Process

### Step One: Imagine and Describe a Situation

Identify a problematic scenario you regularly face. This can involve external events or internal experiences like emotions or thoughts. Document how the situation typically unfolds in sufficient detail.

### Step Two: Select a DBT Skill

Review previous emotion regulation exercises to determine which strategy best addresses your identified situation. Options include problem-solving, emotion exposure, or balancing emotional urges, among others.

### Step Three: Mental Rehearsal

Visualize applying your chosen skill within the problematic scenario. As described in the content: "try to imagine in it in a detailed way and write down the steps in the worksheet provided." Continue practicing mentally until you feel adequately prepared.

## Example Application

The resource includes a detailed example of someone with social anxiety deciding to practice "doing the opposite of emotional urges" when attending gatherings with their spouse's friends, emphasizing that emotions are temporary and skills require practice.

# STOP Skill: DBT Exercise E11

## What STOP Stands For

- S: Stop
- T: Take a step back
- O: Observe
- P: Proceed mindfully

## Overview

The STOP skill is an emotion regulation technique designed to interrupt impulsive reactions during emotionally heightened moments. As the page describes it, this tool "helps you catch yourself in the moment, regulate what's going on in your body, center your mind, and choose the most effective next step."

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### S: Stop

Halt all action—don't speak, move, text, or leave. This interrupts automatic reactions and creates necessary space for more thoughtful responses.

### T: Take a Step Back

Create physical or mental distance. Engage in deep breathing (three to ten breaths recommended) and allow physiological arousal to decrease before proceeding.

### O: Observe

Notice present-moment experiences without judgment—bodily sensations, thoughts, surrounding context, and others' behaviors. Gather information to assess whether you're facing genuine crisis or perceived crisis.

### P: Proceed Mindfully

Choose actions aligned with your goals, values, and long-term wellbeing. Consider: What do you want from this situation? What action helps rather than harms? What would Wise Mind select?

## Practical Example

The page illustrates receiving critical feedback in a meeting. Rather than defending impulsively or shutting down, you pause, breathe, observe your physical reactions and thoughts, then respond constructively: "I'm open to hearing your feedback."

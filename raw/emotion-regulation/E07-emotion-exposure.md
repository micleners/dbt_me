# Emotion Exposure: DBT Exercise E7

## Overview

Emotion Exposure is a Dialectical Behavior Therapy technique designed to help individuals process uncomfortable emotions rather than avoid them. The core premise emphasizes that negative emotional experiences are inevitable, and learning to sit with emotions without blocking them reduces associated problematic behaviors.

## Four-Step Process

### Step One: Identify a Chronic Emotion

Reflect on an emotion you struggle to accept, recognizing it through repetitive problematic behaviors (e.g., frequent conflicts, work difficulties). Document both the emotion and resulting behavior in the worksheet.

### Step Two: Observe When It Arises

Develop awareness throughout your day to notice when this chronic emotion emerges, preparing yourself mentally to apply the subsequent steps.

### Step Three: Sit With the Emotion (5 minutes)

Once the emotion appears:
- Take deep breaths and observe without judgment
- Note intensity level and bodily sensations
- Recognize the temporary, wave-like nature of emotional experiences
- Monitor any intensity changes or secondary emotions

### Step Four: Resist Automatic Reactions

Practice sitting with the emotion without engaging in your typical behavioral response. As the instructions note, "emotions are temporary. They come and go."

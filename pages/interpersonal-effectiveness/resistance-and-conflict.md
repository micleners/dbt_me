Strategies for keeping dialogue going when someone disagrees or opposes you.

1. **Validate their view.** Show understanding without agreeing: "I understand that [their perspective]. On my part, [your perspective]." You acknowledge their feelings and still state your needs.
2. **Repeat your point of view.** When someone floods the conversation with counterarguments, prepare a short, clear statement of your position and calmly repeat it with little variation. Avoid new questions that reopen the debate.
3. **Ask for specifics.** When criticism is vague, ask "What exactly is bothering you about...?" This surfaces the real concern and gives you something concrete to discuss.

Failing to validate what the other person feels, thinks, or needs can make communication and relationships one-sided. See the source page for an example of each skill.

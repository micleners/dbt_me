Modulating intensity sets how strongly you ask for something: assertive, neither passive nor aggressive, scaled to two factors.

1. **Rate the urgency of your need (0–100%).** Must it be dealt with now, or can it wait? How flexible are you on timing and outcome?
2. **Rate the vulnerability (0–100%)** of the other person or relationship. Are they under stress, facing major life challenges, or struggling emotionally in a way that affects how they'll hear you?
3. **Adjust the intensity.** High urgency with low vulnerability may call for a direct approach; lower urgency with higher vulnerability suggests a gentler one.

Best for important requests where you're uncertain how to pitch them. With practice the weighing becomes intuitive rather than explicit. See the source page for the parent-and-son example.

Assertiveness means stating opinions, feelings, and needs clearly, honestly, and respectfully while taking the other person's needs into account. This page covers skills 1–3 of six.

1. **Know your priorities.** Before asking a relationship for anything, get clear on what you want. Use [wise mind](../mindfulness/wise-mind.md) and observe your feelings to find what is missing or wanted. Prompts: do you need support or want to give it? Do you want more time together? Do boundaries need clarifying? Write down your relationship priorities and rate each 1–5 for importance.
2. **Ask for what you want.** Describe the situation factually, without judgment or blame. Express your feelings and state your needs assertively, keeping in mind that the relationship matters as well as the request. Use "I want..." or "I need..." rather than judgments like "You are...".
3. **Negotiate.** Treat both people's needs as valid unless they violate basic interpersonal rights. Show willingness to cooperate and compromise so the exchange rests on mutual trust and respect.

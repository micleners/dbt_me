A structure for communicating needs clearly, respectfully, and confidently: asking for a raise, setting a boundary with family, asking a partner for support. The aim is effectiveness, not manipulation.

**DEAR: what you say**

- **D, Describe** the situation with objective facts only, no judgment. What happened; what is the specific problem?
- **E, Express** your feelings and opinions with "I" language, without blame or criticism.
- **A, Assert** your need or request directly and specifically.
- **R, Reinforce** why it matters: describe the positive outcome or express appreciation.

**MAN: how you say it**

- **M, Mindful.** Stay focused on your objective; don't follow tangents or deflections. If they get defensive, stay grounded and on point. Use the "broken record": calmly repeat the request as needed.
- **A, Appear confident,** even if you don't feel it: steady eye contact, measured tone, upright posture, no apologizing for reasonable needs.
- **N, Negotiate.** Be flexible and willing to collaborate; offer alternatives or look for solutions together.

**Notes from the source FAQ:** It works in writing if the tone stays respectful and free of sarcasm. You won't always get the outcome, but practice builds clarity and integrity. If you tend to lose your words, use GIVE or FAST beforehand to steady yourself. If emotion is too intense to speak, start with ABC PLEASE. If conflict frightens you, pair DEAR MAN with Middle Path.

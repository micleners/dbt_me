Skills 4–6 of the assertiveness framework begun in [Part 1](skills-of-assertiveness.md).

4. **Get information.** Don't assume what the other person thinks. Ask open-ended questions about their thoughts, feelings, and needs to learn where they stand on your request, with present-moment curiosity.
5. **Say no without damaging the relationship.** Decline assertively to set a healthy boundary; this protects both your wellbeing and the relationship over time. Passive or aggressive refusals undercut that.
6. **Act according to your values.** Check two things: the honest motivation behind your request, and the kind of relationship you want to foster. Align what you say with core values such as mental health, integrity, or personal boundaries.

Effective communication acknowledges both yourself and the other person. This exercise moves past assertiveness to the listening side.

1. **Ask questions and reflect back.** Show genuine curiosity about their experience. Ask clarifying and follow-up questions, and reflect what you hear with phrases like "I understand that..." or "I can hear that you feel...".
2. **Body language and identifying with them.** Notice nonverbal cues (posture, eye contact, openness) that may show what they actually feel. Where you can, find shared experiences that help them feel understood.
3. **Accept and encourage participation.** Validate: accept their thoughts and feelings even when you don't fully understand them. Stay nonjudgmental, keep talking through disconnects, and recognize that people experience things differently.

Painful emotions are unavoidable. Learning to sit with them instead of blocking them reduces the problem behaviors that avoidance drives.

1. **Identify a chronic emotion** you struggle to accept. Spot it through the repeated problem behavior it leads to (frequent conflicts, trouble at work). Write down the emotion and the behavior.
2. **Observe when it arises.** Stay alert through the day for when the emotion shows up, ready to use the next steps.
3. **Sit with the emotion (5 minutes).** Breathe deeply and observe without judgment. Note its intensity and the body sensations. Recognize that emotions are temporary and move like waves. Watch for changes in intensity or secondary emotions.
4. **Resist the automatic reaction.** Stay with the emotion without doing your usual behavior. Emotions are temporary; they come and go.

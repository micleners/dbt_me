When you're constantly tired, sick, disconnected, or physically worn down, your capacity to manage emotions drops. ABC PLEASE is a foundational skill that prevents instability by strengthening your baseline.

**ABC: build an emotional foundation**

- **A, Accumulate positive experiences.** Do things that bring joy and connection, starting small (videos, music) while also pursuing meaningful long-term goals.
- **B, Build mastery.** Take on suitable challenges (learning a recipe, finishing an avoided project) for a sense of accomplishment and control.
- **C, Cope ahead.** Mentally rehearse handling future stressors: picture a good outcome and yourself using DBT skills.

**PLEASE: care for the body so it supports the mind**

- **PL, Treat Physical illness.** Address health problems; body and mind depend on each other.
- **E, Eat balanced meals.** Eat regularly and nutritiously to avoid blood sugar swings that feel like emotional crashes.
- **A, Avoid mood-altering substances.** Cut alcohol, drugs, and excess caffeine or sugar.
- **S, Sleep enough.** Keep a consistent sleep schedule; too little rest weakens focus, regulation, and stress response.
- **E, Exercise regularly.** Any movement (walking, stretching, dancing) lowers stress hormones and lifts mood.

**Daily check:** did I do something joyful? Did I challenge myself? Am I avoiding obvious stressors like short sleep or skipped meals? With the basics in place, other DBT skills work better.

People who struggle with emotion regulation often add negative secondary emotions on top of their primary feelings. Self-validation accepts the emotional experience instead of judging it.

1. **Acknowledge.** Name the emotion without judgment. Not "I'm always sad and weak" but "I am feeling sad."
2. **Allow.** Accept that having the emotion is valid: "It is okay to feel the way I do right now", "This emotion is uncomfortable, but it won't hurt me."
3. **Understand.** Look at the circumstances that produced the emotion, sticking to objective facts rather than self-criticism.

Example sequence: "Right now I feel frustrated with myself" → "This feels uncomfortable, but right now it is what it is" → identifying the circumstances (loneliness, depression) without judging yourself.

It's hard at first and gets easier with practice. See the source page for the worksheet.

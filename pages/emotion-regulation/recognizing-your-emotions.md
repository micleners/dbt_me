Linehan's six-step method for understanding how emotions build and spiral. It separates **primary emotions** (the first reaction) from **secondary emotions** (reactions to the primary emotion).

Take an emotionally overwhelming experience and work through:

1. **Describe the situation:** what happened, where, and who was involved.
2. **What caused it:** your thoughts about the cause. How you explain the situation to yourself shapes how you feel about it.
3. **Primary and secondary emotions:** the first emotion, the ones that followed, and any body sensations (tension, fast heartbeat, trembling).
4. **Identify your urges:** what you wanted to do because of the emotions, kept separate from what you actually did.
5. **What you did:** the actions you actually took.
6. **Outcomes:** the longer-term consequences of those actions, and whether they helped.

The aim is to slow escalation and make healthier choices next time. See the source page for the worksheet.

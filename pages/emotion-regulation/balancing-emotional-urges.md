Emotions come paired with natural behaviors, and those automatic responses don't always help. This exercise is about acting opposite to an urge when it's ineffective.

| Emotion | Natural urge | Opposite action |
|---|---|---|
| Anger | Attack verbally or physically | Be polite, gentle, or nice |
| Anxiety/fear | Avoid the situation | Stay in the situation |
| Sadness | Isolate | Seek support and socialize |
| Shame | Hide or avoid people | Approach others and share your thoughts |

1. **Identify the opposite** of each emotion's urge.
2. **Recognize your emotion** and judge whether the automatic urge is effective in this situation.
3. **Do the opposite behavior** on purpose. Feelings aren't always right; they are temporary, programmed mechanisms.
4. **Keep it up.** Real emotional shifts take repetition and time.

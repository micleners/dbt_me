Being effective means actively doing what needs to be done so your needs are met or you move closer to a goal. Getting things done produces positive emotions and breaks the cycle of avoidance and depression. Depression can make even basic tasks feel overwhelming, so start small and build momentum.

**Part one: short-term goals.**

- Daily tasks, including basic self-care (showering, eating).
- Work or study obligations.
- Responsibilities you've been postponing.
- Break each goal into specific, actionable steps.

**Part two: long-term goals.**

- Identify meaningful pursuits that fit your values (the Actions Based on Values worksheet covers broader life areas).
- Choose one priority goal and outline the steps to carry it out.

See the source page for examples and the worksheet.

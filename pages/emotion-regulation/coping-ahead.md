Plan for stressful situations and mentally rehearse the DBT skills you'll use before you face them.

1. **Imagine and describe a situation** you face regularly. It can be external events or internal ones such as emotions or thoughts. Write down in enough detail how it usually unfolds.
2. **Select a DBT skill.** Review the earlier emotion regulation exercises and choose the one that best fits, such as problem solving, emotion exposure, or balancing emotional urges.
3. **Mentally rehearse.** Picture yourself using the skill in the situation in detail, and write the steps on the worksheet. Keep rehearsing until you feel prepared.

See the source page for the social anxiety example.

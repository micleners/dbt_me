A tool for catching yourself in a heated moment, settling your body, centering your mind, and choosing the most effective next move.

- **S, Stop.** Freeze: don't speak, move, text, or leave. This breaks the automatic reaction and makes room for a considered one.
- **T, Take a step back.** Get physical or mental distance. Take deep breaths (three to ten) and let your arousal drop before going on.
- **O, Observe.** Notice what's happening now without judgment: body sensations, thoughts, the context, what others are doing. Use it to tell a real crisis from a perceived one.
- **P, Proceed mindfully.** Choose an action that serves your goals, values, and long-term wellbeing. Ask: what do I want here? What helps rather than harms? What would wise mind choose?

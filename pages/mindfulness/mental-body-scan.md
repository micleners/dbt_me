A 15-minute body-awareness practice, repeated daily for a week. Sit comfortably, feet flat, eyes closed, and take several deep breaths.

1. **Lower body (~5 min).** Start at the feet's contact with the floor — toes, soles, ankles — then move up through lower legs, knees, thighs, pelvis, and where you meet the chair. Notice tired vs. rested, heavy vs. energized.
2. **Upper body (~5 min).** Stomach (calm or nervous, hungry?), lower back, upper back and torso, then hands — fingers, palms, forearms — and up through elbows and upper arms.
3. **Shoulders, neck, head (~5 min).** Common tension spots: shoulders and neck. Then chin, mouth, nose, eyes, ears, and the whole head. Note tightness, headache, or ease.
4. **Document** the most notable, most tense, and most relaxed areas. Over a week, recurring patterns emerge.

Whenever the mind wanders, return to physical sensation without judgment. See the source page for the worksheet.

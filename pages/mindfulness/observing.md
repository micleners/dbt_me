The point is to grow "mindfulness muscles": notice when your attention drifts and bring it back, over and over, without self-criticism. Recommended three times a week.

1. **Find a comfortable, quiet space.** Breathe slowly a few times and let physical tension go.
2. **Pick a small, emotionally neutral object** (a mug, an apple, a notebook) — nothing that carries charged memories.
3. **Observe it with every sense for ~5 minutes**: shape, texture, color, and shine; its weight and feel; any sound or scent.
4. **When the mind wanders** (it will), acknowledge whatever arose — restlessness, boredom, an unrelated thought — without judgment, and return to the object.
5. **Log it afterward**: date, object, its qualities, and any thoughts, emotions, or sensations you noticed.

The goal is twofold: become more present, and get skilled at recognizing distraction and redirecting attention. See the source page for the worksheet and example.

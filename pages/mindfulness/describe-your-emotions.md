Feeling uneasy and then suppressing it tends to make it grow. Describing an emotion specifically helps you see what triggers it and how to manage it. Spend at least 15 minutes; practice daily for a week, then as needed.

1. **Pick an emotion** you feel now or felt recently — positive or negative, but specific (the page offers word lists). Avoid one that's overwhelming.
2. **Draw it.** A concrete image for an abstract feeling (anger as a thunderbolt). Artistic skill is irrelevant; it only has to make sense to you.
3. **Name a fitting action** — the urge the emotion carries (depressed → withdraw; curious → explore).
4. **Rate its intensity** 0–10, or describe it ("as hot as boiling water").
5. **Write the thoughts it produced**, deliberately *not* using emotion words — this separates the emotion from the cognitions it generates ("proud" → "I want to share this with friends").

See the source page for the word lists and worksheet.

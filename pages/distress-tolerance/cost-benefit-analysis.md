A deliberative, out-of-crisis skill: it does not calm you in the moment — it builds the motivation to change a behavior that keeps causing crises.

**Practice (six steps, scored):**

1. **Choose a behavior** you most want to change (e.g., smoking, overeating, impulsive spending).
2. **Costs of the behavior** — every negative consequence of continuing it.
3. **Benefits of the behavior** — the (usually short-term) rewards that keep you doing it.
4. **Costs of the alternative** — the effort, discomfort, and adjustment of changing.
5. **Benefits of the alternative** — vividly imagine the payoff of the healthier action.
6. **Score each item 1–5** for importance. The pattern that emerges: the costs of the behavior and the benefits of the alternative usually outweigh the rest.

Revisit and add to the list over time — changing a habit is a gradual process. See the source page for a full junk-food worked example.

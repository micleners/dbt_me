The first move is noticing you've left the present. Then you deliberately return through four stages:

1. **Describe what you see (~10 min)** — colors, shapes, light, the specific objects around you; linger on each.
2. **Breathing (~5 min)** — follow the breath, counting one to five on the exhale, then let it settle into its natural rhythm.
3. **Mindfulness of the body (~5 min)** — contact points: legs on the chair, feet on the floor, weight of your arms; notice tension or comfort.
4. **Other senses (~5 min)** — open to smells and to near and distant sounds.

Distinct from mindfulness: grounding is for acute distress, mindfulness is the daily practice that builds the awareness grounding relies on. See the source page for examples.

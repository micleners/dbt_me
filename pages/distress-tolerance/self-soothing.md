Simple but effective: sensory comfort nudges you toward a state where you can think again. Because positive feelings are subtler than intense negative ones, this works best as a practiced skill.

**Pick 2–3 per sense:**

- **Sight** — nature, beautiful images or films, a saved collection of pleasing pictures.
- **Hearing** — soothing music, a comforting voice, ambient nature sound, an instrument.
- **Smell** — scented candle, fresh flowers, aromatic cooking, a loved one's scent.
- **Taste** — a favorite meal savored slowly, warm tea or cocoa, fruit eaten mindfully.
- **Touch** — a soft blanket, a pet, a warm bath, a massage.

Document your picks so they're ready in distress. See the source page for the full per-sense list.

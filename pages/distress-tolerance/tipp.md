A first-line intervention for acute overwhelm. Works best when you are too activated to think. Not a fix — a temporary state-shift so wise mind can come back online.

**The four components (do in sequence; you may stop early once relief comes):**

- **T — Temperature.** Regulate heart rate through temperature. For anxiety/panic, use cold (splash face, cool shower, hold ice) to bring an elevated heart rate down. For depression/sadness, use warmth (hot bath, blankets, warm tea).
- **I — Intense exercise.** Burn off the built-up emotional energy with 10–15 minutes of cardio (running, jumping jacks, dancing). Spent energy leaves emotions more balanced.
- **P — Paced breathing.** Slow the body's alarm: inhale through the nose ~4 seconds, exhale through the mouth ~6 seconds (exhale longer than inhale), for 1–2 minutes.
- **P — Progressive muscle relaxation.** Work top to bottom — tense a muscle group ~5 seconds, then release — from upper back and arms down through calves.

See the source page for the caveats and a full worked example.

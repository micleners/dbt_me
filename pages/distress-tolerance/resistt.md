A menu, not a sequence — reach for whichever of the seven techniques fit:

- **R — Reframe** the situation out of black-and-white terms; find the realistic grey.
- **E — Engage** in a distracting, pleasurable activity (your prepared list).
- **S — Someone else:** shift focus outward by helping or listening to another person.
- **I — Intense sensations:** safe, strong sensory input (ice cube, cold shower) — Linehan notes holding ice can replace self-harm urges.
- **S — Shut it out:** leave the triggering environment, or mentally box the problem for later; solve it now only if solvable now.
- **T — Neutral thoughts:** count breaths, name colors, repeat a steadying phrase.
- **T — Take a break:** rest or step back from obligations (use sparingly).

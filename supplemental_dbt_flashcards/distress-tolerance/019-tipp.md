## Summary

A crisis switches on the sympathetic nervous system (fight-or-flight), which can leave you shaky, tense, or agitated. That stress state can fuel the pressure or urgency behind impulsive behavior. The TIPP skills aim to turn off the stress response and turn on the parasympathetic system (rest-and-digest).

## Practice

The TIPP skills are:

- **T**ip Your Temperature (Card 20)
- **I**ntense Exercise (Card 21)
- **P**aced Breathing (Card 22)
- **P**aired Muscle Relaxation (Card 23)

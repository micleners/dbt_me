## Summary

Distressing emotions tend to make people somewhat self-centered. Wanting to focus on problems until they are solved is natural, but rumination can make distress worse. Doing nice things for others can pull you out of this self-focused emotional storm, at least temporarily.

## Practice

Suggestions: volunteer at a local retirement home, soup kitchen, or animal shelter; pick up trash in your local park; write a nice letter to a soldier, an incarcerated person, or a friend; buy or make a gift for a loved one; call your congressperson about an issue you care about; donate goods to those in need.

## Notes

- The first "C" of ACCEPTS (`distress-tolerance/accepts`).
- Several examples need lead time (volunteering, gifts); in a crisis, the letter, call, or donation are the quicker ones.

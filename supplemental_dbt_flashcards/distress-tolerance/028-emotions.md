## Summary

Intense painful emotions can become all-consuming and push us toward things that keep them around. To interrupt this cycle, distract yourself with activities that make you feel an emotion different from the one upsetting you, without making you feel worse. Do anything that usually evokes emotions for you.

## Practice

Suggestions: watch standup comedy, cute puppy videos, horror films, or news reports that make you feel impassioned or energized. Listen to sad, moody music or energizing, uplifting music. Read a romance novel, a serial killer mystery, or a fun, sad, or empowering memoir.

## Notes

- The "E" of ACCEPTS (`distress-tolerance/accepts`), where it appears as "opposite" emotions.
- The card's test is "different from the current emotion, and not worse", so the right pick depends on the emotion being distracted from; a sad song does not suit someone already sad.

## Summary

Practicing One-mindfully (Card 7) can be very hard during an emotional crisis, but it can also be strongly calming and grounding. Current pain gets worse when we worry about how things might keep going wrong later or ruminate on everything that has gone wrong before. Keeping attention on only this moment can reduce suffering.

## Practice

- Take one long, deep breath and notice its physical sensations.
- Say "Just this moment" out loud to yourself, or use Coping Statements for Overwhelm (Card 58).
- Immerse yourself in distracting Activities (Card 25) or physical Sensations (Card 31).
- Do a guided meditation.
- Do a Body Scan (Card 13).

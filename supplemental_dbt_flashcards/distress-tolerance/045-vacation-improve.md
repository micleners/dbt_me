## Summary

Everyone needs a break sometimes. Choosing avoidance *mindfully* means taking a brief "Vacation" from stressful realities: on purpose, retreat, escape, or let yourself be cared for for a short time. Afterward, use Problem-Solve (Card 78) to work out how to reduce the stressors that make you need time away.

## Practice

- Close your eyes for five minutes.
- Take a nap.
- Turn your phone off for an hour, a day, or a weekend, after letting others know as needed.
- Take a sick day or personal day from work.
- Ask for help, or hand responsibilities to someone else.
- Go for a drive with the windows down or your favorite music on.

- Distinct from `distress-tolerance/distracting-activities` and `distress-tolerance/accepts`: the point is a mindful, bounded withdrawal, not attention redirection. The "short period" limit is what separates it from avoidance as a problem behavior.

## Summary

A crisis is a highly stressful, short-term situation you want resolved fast, which often brings impulsive urges toward behaviors that may hurt you. This card is a check for whether you are in one.

## Practice

Ask yourself:

- Are you past your skills breakdown point (see booklet pages 11 to 12)?
- Are you overwhelmed by emotion or physiological agitation?
- Are you having intense urges to do something that may harm you?

If any answer is yes, use Crisis Survival skills (Cards 17 to 46). If all are no, access Wise Mind (Card 9) and use whichever skills help you act most Effectively (Card 8).

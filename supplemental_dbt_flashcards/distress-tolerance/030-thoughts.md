## Summary

Distressing thoughts can cause painful emotions long after a crisis situation occurs. Filling your brain with neutral thoughts is an effective technique for interrupting negative thoughts and keeping your painful emotions from intensifying.

## Practice

Suggestions: label all the things around you that are green (or any color). Recite state capitals, multiplication tables, or memorized poems. Play logic, word, or math puzzles. Count the number of birds, people, tiles, trees, or other things around you. Sing a song in your head. Name as many animals, foods, or countries as you can in one minute.

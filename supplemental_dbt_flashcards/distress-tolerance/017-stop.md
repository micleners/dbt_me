## Summary

STOP is the first Crisis Survival skill to reach for when trying to reduce impulsive behaviors.

## Practice

1. **Stop:** Pause and take a deep breath before you react.
2. **Take a step back:** Physically remove yourself from the impulsive urge. As needed, walk away, turn off your phone, clasp your hands together, sit or lie down, etc.
3. **Observe:** Notice and name the facts of your situation, along with your emotions, thoughts, and urges. Use Self-Validation (Card 114).
4. **Proceed mindfully:** Act on purpose. Use Distress Tolerance skills (Cards 16 to 59), Problem-Solve (Card 78), or otherwise act Effectively (Card 8). Ask which behaviors would move you toward your goals or help you feel better without creating problems.

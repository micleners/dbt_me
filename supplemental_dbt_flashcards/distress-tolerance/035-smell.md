## Summary

Consider which scents bring you pleasure, warmth, or happiness, and how Smell could calm you in a crisis or a painful moment. Pick scents to breathe in that you personally find soothing.

## Practice

Suggestions: wear a favorite perfume, cologne, or scented lotion. Shower or bathe for a clean, fresh smell, or use scented bath products you like. Make "stovetop potpourri" by simmering herbs, cinnamon, citrus, cloves, or pine needles in a pot of water. Light scented candles or incense. Open the windows to let in fresh air. Bake cookies or another dessert. Smell a bouquet of fresh flowers. Breathe in the scent of a loved one, with their consent.

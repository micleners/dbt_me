## Summary

Filling your attention with an engaging or pleasurable hobby or task can distract you from a distressing situation. Distraction through Activities can help you avoid impulsive behaviors and sometimes may even make you feel better temporarily. Do anything that absorbs your full attention; ideally, choose something enjoyable or pleasurable for you.

## Practice

Suggestions: go on a bike ride or walk outside, watch TV or YouTube, read a book, clean your room, play with makeup, knit, paint, color, or do other crafts, cook a new or favorite recipe.

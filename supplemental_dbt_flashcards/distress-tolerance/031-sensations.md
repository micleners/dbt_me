## Summary

Intense emotions can bring racing thoughts, disorientation, or overwhelm. Intense physical sensations that don't physically harm you can help you cope by giving you a stable anchor during emotional overload. Because sensations are short-lived, try several in a row, or move straight into another ACCEPTS skill afterward.

## Practice

Suggestions: take a cold shower, chew ice, or hold an ice pack. Eat a small amount of vinegar, hot sauce, spicy peppers, sour candy, lemon wedges, ginger chews, or fermented food. Listen to loud music. Jump up and down, or hug yourself very tightly.

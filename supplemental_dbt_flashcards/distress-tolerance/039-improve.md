## Summary

All crisis survival skills (Cards 16 to 46) help when you are intensely upset and past your skills breakdown point. Some of them ease coping when you are stuck in a distressing situation you can't change right now. These skills help you IMPROVE painful moments.

## Practice

The seven options, with their deck cards:

1. Use mental **I**magery (Card 40)
2. Find **M**eaning (Card 41)
3. Use **P**rayer or spiritual practices (Card 42)
4. Take **R**elaxing Actions (Card 43)
5. Do only **O**ne Thing in the Moment (Card 44)
6. Take a brief **V**acation (Card 45)
7. Rethink through Self-**E**ncouragement (Card 46)

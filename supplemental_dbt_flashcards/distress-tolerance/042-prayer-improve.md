## Summary

In this skill, "Prayer" covers any spiritual practice that feels meaningful to you; being religious is not required. Turning attention to your connection with God, other people, nature, or some larger cosmic experience can make your own emotional burden feel lighter.

## Practice

- Pray, asking for strength, wisdom, or openness.
- Go to religious services.
- Recite the Serenity Prayer or mantras.
- Connect with nature: hug a tree, lie on the grass, or something similar.
- Practice loving, respecting, and thanking your body or your ancestors.
- Journal about your deepest emotions and experiences.
- Make art.

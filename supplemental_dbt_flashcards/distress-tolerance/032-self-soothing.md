## Summary

Emotional crises wear you out. Deliberately nurturing and physically comforting yourself during stressful times can greatly reduce your suffering and build your emotional resilience.

## Practice

Do things that bring you pleasure or relief through your body's senses: the "typical five" of Vision, Hearing, Smell, Taste, and Touch (Cards 33 to 37), plus Proprioception and the Vestibular System (Card 38). Self-soothe One-mindfully (Card 7). Consider combining several sense experiences to immerse yourself fully.

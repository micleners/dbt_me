## Summary

Consider which movements or body positions feel good to you, and how motion or pressure could calm you in a crisis or a difficult moment. Pick something to experience that you personally find soothing.

## Practice

Suggestions: bounce, jump, shake, or sway. Give yourself a massage. Ride the swings at a park. Use a weighted blanket, ask a loved one for a tight hug, or wrap yourself snugly in a blanket. Curl up in a soft chair, or lie on a hard floor. Chew gum or hard candy. Hang off the edge of your bed, or lie with your back on the floor and your legs up on the bed. Use fidget toys. Stretch.

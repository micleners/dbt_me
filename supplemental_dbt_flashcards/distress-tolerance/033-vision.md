## Summary

Consider which sights bring you pleasure, warmth, or happiness, and how Vision could calm you in a crisis or a painful moment. Pick something to look at that you personally find soothing.

## Practice

Suggestions: visit a museum or browse art online. Stargaze. Watch running water, a candle flame, a sunrise or sunset, or ocean waves, in person or on YouTube. Look through photos of loved ones, ancestors, or favorite celebrities. Dim the lights. Watch videos of colorful birds or animals. Study mandalas or other spiritual symbols. Look at the architecture in your neighborhood. Watch wind moving through trees, or clouds drifting across the sky.

- The Sight sense of `distress-tolerance/self-soothing`, which lists nature, beautiful images or films, and a saved collection of pleasing pictures. The deck calls it Vision.
- Watching clouds drift echoes the Clouds in the Sky version of `mindfulness/thought-defusion`, though the card uses it only as a pleasant sight.

## Summary

Distraction can make painful moments easier to tolerate. Filling your attention with things unrelated to the crisis keeps painful emotions from being repeatedly retriggered, and the right distraction can help you avoid impulsive behaviors.

The "Wise Mind ACCEPTS" skill set provides options for distraction.

## Practice

The seven options, with their deck cards:

1. Activities (Card 25)
2. Contributing (Card 26)
3. Comparisons (Card 27)
4. Emotions (Card 28)
5. Pushing Away (Card 29)
6. Thoughts (Card 30)
7. Sensations (Card 31)

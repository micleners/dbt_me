## Summary

Consider which tastes bring you pleasure, warmth, or happiness, and how Taste could calm you in a crisis or a painful moment. Pick something to eat or drink that you personally find soothing.

## Practice

Suggestions: eat your comfort food (sweet, savory, and/or spicy) slowly and One-mindfully (Card 7). Eat a meal that brings back happy memories. Drink herbal tea, fresh juice, a smoothie, or hot chocolate. Spend a full day eating only food you enjoy. Taste-test different varieties of a favorite food or drink (e.g., chocolate, seltzer, kimchi, fancy cheeses).

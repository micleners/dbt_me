## Summary

Exercise switches on the body's stress response, and stopping to rest afterward often brings on the relaxation response. Exercising on purpose can therefore lower stress, acting like a "hard reset" of your nervous system. For the biggest effect, exercise for at least 20 minutes, hard enough that you can't say more than one full sentence aloud at a time. Rest right afterward.

## Practice

Suggestions: jumping jacks, air punching, fast yoga flows, or speed walking or jogging.

**Caution:** If you have cardiac or other health problems, check with a medical professional before trying this skill.

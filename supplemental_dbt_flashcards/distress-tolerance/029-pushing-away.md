## Summary

When life is overwhelmingly painful, the most effective coping strategy can be to ignore or "push away" the situation. Automatically coping with painful emotions by suppressing them works, but it also causes more problems and pain in the long term if it is the only skill you use.

Pushing Away is therefore a temporary solution to choose mindfully as a first step, before turning to Radical Acceptance (Card 47), Problem-Solving (Card 78), or other skills. Access Wise Mind (Card 9) to know when to stop Pushing Away.

## Practice

Suggestions: deny reality or refuse to think and talk about it. Block thoughts or images. Imagine your problems as being behind a wall or in a box way high up on a shelf.

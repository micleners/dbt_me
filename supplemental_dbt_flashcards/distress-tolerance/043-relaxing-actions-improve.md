## Summary

Hard periods commonly leave the body agitated, tired, or tense. Focusing on Relaxing Actions helps sustain emotional well-being and reduce suffering: do whatever you find soothing or relieving.

## Practice

- Use Paced Breathing (Card 22) and Paired Muscle Relaxation (Card 23).
- Meditate, stretch, or do yoga.
- Drink tea or warm milk.
- Take a bath with Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate).
- Use a foam roller, an acupressure mat, or another self-massage tool.
- Sit outside in nature.
- Crochet, draw, paint, or dance.
- Spend time on your special interests.
- Light a candle.
- Listen to relaxing music.

## Summary

Your heart rate drops slightly each time you breathe out, and deep breathing comes naturally when you're very relaxed. Deliberately deepening your breath and lengthening your exhales can calm your body.

## Practice

1. Breathe in through your nose for four seconds and out through your mouth for six seconds, or use whatever pace you prefer as long as each exhale is longer than the inhale.
2. Rest a hand on your belly; with deep breathing, it rises and falls with each breath.
3. Keep going for one to ten minutes, or as long as you like.

## Notes

- Same 4-in / 6-out pattern as T10's first P. T10 says 1–2 minutes; the card allows one to ten minutes or longer.
- The belly check is the diaphragmatic cue; compare `mindfulness/mindful-breathing`, which uses the breath as an attention anchor rather than to slow the heart.

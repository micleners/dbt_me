## Summary

Consider which textures or tactile sensations bring you pleasure, warmth, or happiness, and how Touch could calm you in a crisis or a painful moment. Pick something to feel that you personally find soothing.

## Practice

Suggestions: put on your softest clothes. Wrap up in a fuzzy blanket, or wear slippers. Pet a real animal, a stuffed animal, or another soft thing. Run your hand over marble, wood, leather, or another smooth material. Take a cool shower or a warm bath. Massage your temples, feet, or hands. Put a warm or cold compress on your forehead, lower abdomen, or the back of your neck.

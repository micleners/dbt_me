## Summary

The "mammalian dive reflex" is an automatic response that slows your heart rate when you're underwater. When you're upset, you can trigger it on purpose to calm down.

## Practice

1. Fill a large bowl or sink with cold water, and add a handful of ice cubes if you have them.
2. Put your whole face in the water and hold your breath as long as you can. Repeat as needed.

**Another option:** Hold your breath while covering your eyes and nose with a flexible ice pack wrapped in a wet towel.

**Caution:** If you have heart or other health problems, check with a medical professional before trying this skill.

## Summary

In the middle of an emotional crisis it can be hard to remember why you want to stop certain impulsive behaviors (crisis urges). To help you act Effectively (Card 8), work out the Pros (positive consequences) and Cons (negative consequences) in advance, so you can look them over during a crisis.

## Practice

1. Draw four columns on a sheet of paper and write your crisis urge at the top.
2. In columns one and two, list the Pros and Cons of acting on the crisis urge. Be honest, and include both short- and long-term consequences.
3. In columns three and four, list the Pros and Cons of not acting on the urge and using skills instead.
4. Circle the two to three consequences that matter most to you.

## Notes

- This is Linehan's Pros and Cons crisis survival skill; T1 Cost Benefit Analysis is the repo's version of the same four-quadrant grid.
- Differences: T1 targets a recurring behavior and scores items 1–5; the card targets a crisis urge, is written ahead of time for use during the crisis, and uses circling instead of scores.
- T1's `when_not_to_use` says "not mid-crisis"; the card agrees on building the list when calm but adds reviewing it mid-crisis. Worth a note on T1.

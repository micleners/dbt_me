## Summary

Meaning means "making lemonade out of lemons," or tying what you go through to a larger purpose. You should not invalidate yourself by denying the stressful or unwanted parts of a painful experience, but deliberately turning your attention to its positive aspects can reduce suffering.

## Practice

- Name a beneficial outcome or larger meaning in a hard life event or circumstance. Ask yourself:
  - What has living through it taught you?
  - What experience you want has it opened room for?
  - In what ways do you see yourself more positively for having gone through it?
- Read books, listen to podcasts, or watch films about spiritual values, resilience, or how other people have overcome trauma.

## Summary

Consider which sounds bring you pleasure, warmth, or happiness, and how Hearing could calm you in a crisis or a painful moment. Pick something to listen to that you personally find soothing.

## Practice

Suggestions: hum or sing to yourself. Switch on a white noise machine or app. Listen to chanting, singing bowls, or sound healing playlists. Listen to water, sitting beside a river or running the shower. Visit a local park to hear the birds. Watch YouTube videos of nature sounds, purring cats, relaxed coffee shops, or ASMR (autonomous sensory meridian response) recordings. Listen to music, trying familiar against new songs, upbeat pop against spa music, and loud against soft volume.

## Summary

Describing means putting what you Observe (Card 2) into words, narrating what reaches you through your senses. Aim to be specific and factual, leaving out judgments, assumptions, and opinions. Name thoughts as thoughts, emotions as emotions, sensations as sensations, and behaviors as behaviors.

## Practice

- Do a Body Scan (Card 13).
- Draw something, then describe it to a friend and see whether they can reproduce the drawing without looking at it.
- At a park, describe one detail you notice about each bird you see.
- Describe the bodily sensations that come with happiness or any other emotion.
- Several times a day, finish the sentence: "I just had the thought . . ."

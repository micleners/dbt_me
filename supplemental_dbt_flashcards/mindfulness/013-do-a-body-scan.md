## Summary

A body scan grounds you by applying Observe (deck card 2) and Describe (deck card 3) to physical sensations, one body region at a time.

## Practice

1. Sit or lie down comfortably and take three deep breaths.
2. Observe your feet, then Describe what you feel in your toes, arches, and ankles. Notice whether you feel your feet against the floor, your shoes or socks, or other body parts, and whether you feel cold or heat, pain or tension, tingling or pulsing, or anything else.
3. When ready, shift attention to your lower legs. Observe and Describe what your calves and knees are in contact with and what they feel.
4. Continue the same way through the rest of the body: thighs, pelvic area, abdomen, chest, hands, arms, shoulders, neck, jaw/tongue/lips, cheeks/nose, eyes/forehead, and scalp.
5. End with a few deep breaths.

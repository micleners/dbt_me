## Summary

Observing the Breath is among the most often recommended mindfulness exercises, probably because the breath is always there.

## Practice

Close your eyes, take three deep breaths, then breathe normally. Pick one of four options:

1. Count your breaths, focusing on the physical sensations at the tip of your nose, the back of your throat, or your abdomen.
2. Say "inhale" as you breathe in and "exhale" as you breathe out.
3. Breathe in time with music.
4. Focus on the pauses between inhales and exhales.

The practice is simple but not easy. If you can't do it without panicking or dissociating, open your eyes and focus on external signs of breathing (e.g., watching your belly move), or use other External Mindfulness Ideas (deck card 14).

## Summary

Nonjudgmentally means letting go of judgments: interpretations or assumptions about reality that the senses can't directly observe. Because the brain naturally sorts and evaluates, this is among the hardest DBT skills and takes practice. Take care not to judge yourself for judging.

## Practice

- Describe (Card 3) an emotional event using only the facts.
- Describe a hard decision, noting which options could help or harm based on your goals.
- Describe something you hate, labeling each emotion and opinion as it comes up.
- Describe a person nonjudgmentally, e.g. "He kept invalidating me, so I ended the friendship" instead of "He's a narcissistic jerk."

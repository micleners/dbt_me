## Summary

Wise Mind is the DBT name for inner wisdom or intuition, which helps with life decisions and with knowing your authentic self. It joins two other states of mind. Emotion Mind is "hot" and impulsive, attending only to feelings, passions, and values and urges that shift with mood. Reasonable Mind is "cool", attending only to facts, logistics, and goal-directed calculation. Wise Mind is the synthesis of these two dialectical extremes.

## Practice

- Over the course of the day, notice whether you're in Emotion Mind, Reasonable Mind, or Wise Mind.

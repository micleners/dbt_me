## Summary

Effectively means keeping your goals in mind, acting skillfully, doing what works in the situation at hand, and participating fully on that basis.

## Practice

- As you go about your life, ask yourself, "Is this effective?"
- When feeling hostile, or recalling a recent conflict, notice any urge to be "right" instead of effective toward your goals.
- Notice thoughts about how things "should" be, and act on how they actually are.
- Act from Wise Mind (Card 9) or practice Skillful Means (Card 11).
- Practice Willingness (Card 48).

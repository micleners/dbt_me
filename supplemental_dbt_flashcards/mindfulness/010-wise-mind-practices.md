## Summary

Getting to your inner wisdom can be hard, but as with most skills, finding Wise Mind becomes easier with practice.

## Practice

- **Ask on the breath:** using Observing the Breath (deck card 12), ask "What is Wise Mind?" on the in-breath and listen for an answer on the out-breath.
- **Two lists:** list what your emotions want you to do and what logic requires. Look for overlap, or a way to combine the wants and the requirements into a Wise Mind decision.
- **Recall:** remember how you felt in Wise Mind before, and ask what might bring that feeling back now.

## Notes

- The two-list practice compresses M8's "identify your emotions" and "gather information" steps followed by "integrate".
- Asking on the in-breath and listening on the out-breath matches the "wise mind breathing" exercise in Linehan's DBT Skills Training Manual; the repo has no equivalent.

## Summary

Observing means simply attending to what is going on inside your body, around you, or both. Use your senses and try not to attach words to what you notice; just notice. Expect wordless noticing to feel nearly impossible at first. You can practice by narrowing attention to a single thing or by widening it to everything in your awareness.

## Practice

- Observe the Breath (Card 12).
- Eat one piece of food very slowly, exploring it with each of your senses.
- Watch a sunset or sunrise change color.
- Get a massage and attend to the physical sensations.
- Take a walk and attend to the sounds around you.

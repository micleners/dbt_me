## Summary

Participating means throwing yourself into whatever the present moment holds. Done fully, you act intuitively and experience "flow": complete absorption in the activity, forgetting yourself, and a sense of wider connection.

## Practice

- Dance, sing, or play an instrument, with or without music.
- Walk without your phone or headphones.
- Play a video game you enjoy.
- Take a class in breathwork, improv comedy, ecstatic dance, or laughter yoga.
- Join a new social group and put your attention on taking part instead of on self-doubt or anxious thoughts.
- Do activities that Accumulate Positives in the Short Term (Card 89) or Build Mastery (Card 93).

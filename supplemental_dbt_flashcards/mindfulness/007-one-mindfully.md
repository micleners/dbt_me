## Summary

One-mindfully means staying in the present by deliberately focusing on one thing at a time. The point isn't to avoid distraction; it's to notice when you've become distracted and keep returning to the focus you chose. No multitasking.

## Practice

- Participate (Card 4) in an everyday activity such as walking, eating, or showering, doing it deliberately slowly and noticing every physical sensation and movement.
- During any activity (e.g. cleaning, watching TV, meditating), set a short timer to go off at intervals. Each time it sounds, bring your attention back to the activity if it has drifted. Lengthen the interval as your attention improves.

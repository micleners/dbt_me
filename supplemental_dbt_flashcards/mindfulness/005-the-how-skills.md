## Summary

Mindfulness means being present without adding to, clinging to, judging, or avoiding what is happening. The three "How" skills describe the manner in which you carry out Observing (Card 2), Describing (Card 3), and Participating (Card 4).

## Practice

- **Nonjudgmentally** (Card 6): acknowledge everything in the present moment exactly as it is, without rating it "good" or "bad" and without treating opinions as facts.
- **One-mindfully** (Card 7): do one thing, and only that thing, with full attention.
- **Effectively** (Card 8): do what works, grounded in reality and your goals.

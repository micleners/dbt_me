## Summary

Mindfulness isn't limited to meditation; any activity, including everyday ones, can serve as practice. DBT defines mindfulness as being in the present moment as it actually is, without adding to it, clinging to it, judging it, or avoiding it. Even brief practices can strengthen your ability to use the skills and reduce emotional suffering.

## Practice

The three "What" skills are what you do when practicing mindfulness:

1. **Observe** (Card 2): notice what you see, hear, smell, taste, feel, and directly experience.
2. **Describe** (Card 3): put what you observe into words.
3. **Participate** (Card 4): throw yourself fully into whatever you're doing in the present moment.

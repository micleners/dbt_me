## Summary

Skillful Means is a version of Wise Mind (deck card 9) that dialectically joins Doing Mind and Being Mind. Doing Mind is ambitious and goal-oriented, focused on solving problems and achieving. Being Mind is open and present-oriented, focused on "stopping to smell the roses" right now. Skillful Means asks you to work hard toward goals while releasing the need to achieve any particular result. It is a "Middle Path" between acceptance and change: between enjoying your life as it is and working to make it different.

## Practice

- Ask: "Am I participating effectively in Wise Mind right now? How can I better balance Doing Mind with Being Mind today?"

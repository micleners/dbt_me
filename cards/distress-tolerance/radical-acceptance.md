Resisting what has already happened — through anger, blame, or rumination — doesn't remove the pain; it adds suffering on top of it. Radical acceptance is acknowledging reality as it is so you can stop fighting it and move forward.

**Practice (accepting an event):**

1. **Choose an event** you struggle to accept (start with something manageable if it's trauma-related).
2. **What caused it?** State the factual chain without judgment or self-blame.
3. **Accept the feelings** the event brings — notice the emotions and body sensations, and let them be, without fighting.
4. **Make a proactive plan** — for minor events, acceptance itself may be enough; for larger ones, build an action plan (use Wise Mind if unsure).

**Coping statements** help, e.g. *"I can't change what has already happened,"* *"This moment is exactly as it should be even though I don't like it."* See the source page for the full list.

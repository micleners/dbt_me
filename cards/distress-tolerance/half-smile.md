Based on facial feedback: the body signals the brain, not just the reverse. A subtle softening of the mouth tells your nervous system it's safe to relax — without pretending the pain isn't there.

**Practice (four steps):**

1. **Form it** — relaxed posture, natural breath, corners of the mouth lifting *slightly*; not a grin. Hold a few breaths.
2. **Practice neutral** — when calm, hold it a full minute focused on the breath; 1 minute, five times a week.
3. **Apply under mild distress** — traffic, a passive-aggressive comment: pause, breathe, half smile, relax the face, let the emotion be.
4. **Reflect** — what shifted in the body? Did intensity ease? Did you interrupt the automatic reaction?

Effects are often subtle; consistency builds the brain-body coordination. See the source page for the FAQ on why it works.

A preparation skill: you build the list *before* a crisis so it is ready when you cannot think clearly.

**Practice:**

1. **List things you enjoy** — hobbies feasible on short notice (reading, a walk, music, pets). Start with five.
2. **Scan the suggested list** and check what appeals (cooking, exercise, museums, games, calling someone, and dozens more).
3. **Build your personal list of 15–20, and be specific** — not "listen to music" but a named playlist prepared in advance. Specificity makes the list usable mid-distress.

If depression makes nothing sound enjoyable, rate anticipated enjoyment 0–100 before, and actual after — the real number is often higher. See the source page for the full activity list.

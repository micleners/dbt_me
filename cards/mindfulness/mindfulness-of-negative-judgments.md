We judge constantly, often unconsciously, and negative judgments make us feel bad and less able to manage the situation. This exercise builds awareness of them; the next one (Letting Go) reframes them. Over one week, whenever a negative judgment arises, log four things:

1. **Situation** — what triggered it, where you were, the time of day.
2. **Judgment** — the exact words that crossed your mind ("I hate my monotonous job...").
3. **Resulting emotion** — how you felt ("sad and depressed," "angry and annoyed").
4. **Outcome** — what you did next, and whether the emotion got in the way ("got quiet and worked less effectively," "said something snide").

The aim isn't to stop all judging — some judgments assess real safety or performance — but to notice the unproductive negative ones and, later, loosen them. See the source page for the worksheet and example entries.

Breathing is automatic, always with you, and a powerful anchor: "if you feel anxious or if you are starting to panic, you breathe." It redirects attention when the mind clouds and activates the body's relaxation response. Try one version daily for a week; once familiar, use it anywhere.

- **Counting Breaths (10 min).** On each exhale, count "one... two... three... four... five," then repeat — without changing your natural rhythm. When the mind wanders, return to counting.
- **Sensations of Breathing (10 min).** Breathe normally and focus on the feel of air entering the nostrils and the physical sensation of each breath.
- **Diaphragmatic Breathing (10 min).** A hand on the belly; breathe in through the nose and feel it rise, exhale and feel it fall, keeping a steady, relaxed rhythm.

For each, note your mood before and after. See the source page for the worksheet.

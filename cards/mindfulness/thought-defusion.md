Drawn from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, defusion puts space between you and your thoughts. Over-attachment to negative thoughts adds needless suffering; distance makes them less believable and easier to tolerate. Practice 2–3 times a week or as needed. Three imagery versions, ~10 minutes each:

- **Words in the Sand** — picture a desert; see each thought written in the sand and the wind blowing it away as you exhale.
- **Fish in the Ocean** — picture a sunlit ocean floor; a fish carries each thought away, leaving the water clear again.
- **Clouds in the Sky** — picture a windy sky; write each thought on a cloud in a word or two and watch it float off.

The core move is progressive distancing, e.g. *"why did I say something so stupid"* becomes *"I'm noticing a thought that what I said was stupid,"* then *"I'm noticing I'm having another judgment about myself — this judgment is not a fact."* Then let the image carry it off.

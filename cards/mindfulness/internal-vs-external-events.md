Over-focusing internally can amplify distress; over-focusing externally can bury emotions. The aim is balanced awareness of both.

**Step 1 — Find your orientation.** Check which pulls your attention more: internal items (thoughts, emotions, physical sensations, urges, images and memories) or external items (what you see, smells, sounds, people, the environment). Then deliberately practice the *other* side.

**Part A — Bring awareness outward (~10 min):** spatial awareness (stand, feel the room's size and your place in it), then vision (light, color, objects, scanning all directions), sound (near/far, loud/quiet), smell, and finally integrate all senses without favoring one.

**Part B — Bring awareness inward (~10 min):** observe thoughts entering and leaving without attachment; identify your underlying mood and any strong emotions; scan the body for tension, fatigue, or needs.

Record what you noticed and how you felt afterward. See the source page for the checklist worksheet.
